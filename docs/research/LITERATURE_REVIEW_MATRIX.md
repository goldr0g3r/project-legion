## Review questions

- What plant fidelity is used?
- Are currents randomized or deterministic?
- Is evaluation simulation-only?
- Is a classical baseline included?
- Are failure rates reported?
- Are training and evaluation conditions separated?
- Is hardware timing measured?
- Are code and parameters reproducible?
